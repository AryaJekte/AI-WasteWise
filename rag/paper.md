# Paper Waste

## What is it?
Paper is a commonly used material found in books, newspapers, documents, notebooks, and paper packaging.

## Common Examples
- Newspapers
- Office paper
- Notebooks
- Magazines
- Paper sheets
- Paper packaging

## Before Recycling
- Keep paper clean and dry.
- Remove plastic covers, clips, or other non-paper materials when possible.
- Avoid mixing heavily contaminated paper with clean recyclable paper.
- Flatten paper packaging when appropriate.

## Reuse Ideas
- Use one-sided paper for notes or drafts.
- Reuse paper for crafts.
- Use suitable paper packaging for wrapping or organizing items.

## Disposal Guidance
Clean and dry paper is commonly recyclable, but accepted paper types vary between local recycling systems.

Check your local recycling guidelines before disposal.
