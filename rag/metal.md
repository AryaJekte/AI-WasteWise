# Metal Waste

## What is it?
Metal is a durable material commonly used for cans, containers, packaging, tools, and household products.

## Common Examples
- Aluminum cans
- Steel food cans
- Metal containers
- Metal packaging
- Small metal household items

## Before Recycling
- Empty the container.
- Remove food or other residues when appropriate.
- Rinse cans if needed.
- Keep metal separate from general waste when required by your local system.
- Avoid putting hazardous or sharp metal items into ordinary recycling unless your local guidelines allow it.

## Reuse Ideas
- Reuse suitable metal containers for storage.
- Repurpose cans for organizing small objects.
- Use suitable metal packaging for craft projects.

## Disposal Guidance
Many metal cans and containers can be recycled because metals can be recovered and reused.

However, accepted metal items vary by local recycling systems.

Check your local recycling guidelines before disposal.
