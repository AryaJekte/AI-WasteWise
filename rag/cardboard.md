# Cardboard Waste

## What is it?
Cardboard is a paper-based material commonly used for boxes, packaging, cartons, and shipping containers.

## Common Examples
- Shipping boxes
- Cardboard cartons
- Packaging boxes
- Cereal boxes
- Paperboard packaging

## Before Recycling
- Remove plastic, foam, tape, and other non-cardboard materials when possible.
- Flatten large boxes to save space.
- Keep cardboard reasonably clean and dry.
- Do not mix heavily contaminated cardboard with clean recyclable material.

## Reuse Ideas
- Reuse boxes for storage.
- Use cardboard for organizing items.
- Repurpose cardboard for crafts or packaging.

## Disposal Guidance
Clean and dry cardboard is commonly recyclable, but accepted materials vary by local waste collection systems.

Check your local recycling guidelines before disposal.
