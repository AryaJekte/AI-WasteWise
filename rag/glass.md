# Glass Waste

## What is it?
Glass is a rigid material commonly used for bottles, jars, containers, and other packaging.

## Common Examples
- Glass bottles
- Glass jars
- Food containers
- Beverage bottles
- Glass packaging

## Before Recycling
- Empty the container.
- Rinse it when appropriate.
- Remove leftover food or liquid.
- Keep glass separate if your local collection system requires it.
- Handle broken glass carefully.

## Reuse Ideas
- Reuse suitable jars as storage containers.
- Use clean glass containers for organizing household items.
- Repurpose suitable jars for crafts or decoration.

## Disposal Guidance
Many glass bottles and jars can be recycled, but accepted glass types vary between recycling systems.

Do not assume that mirrors, ceramics, drinking glasses, or heat-resistant glass are accepted with ordinary container glass.

Check your local recycling guidelines before disposal.

## Safety Note
Never handle broken glass with bare hands. Wrap or contain sharp pieces safely according to local waste guidance.
