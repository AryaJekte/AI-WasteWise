# General Trash

## What is it?
General trash refers to waste that does not fit into the recyclable material categories supported by this AI WasteWise system.

## Common Examples
- Mixed-material waste
- Heavily contaminated items
- Items that are not accepted by the local recycling system
- Certain damaged household items

## Before Disposal
- Check whether any part of the item can be reused or recycled.
- Separate recyclable components when practical.
- Keep hazardous materials out of ordinary household waste.
- Follow local waste collection instructions.

## Reuse Ideas
Before throwing an item away, consider whether it can be repaired, reused, donated, or repurposed.

## Disposal Guidance
Do not automatically assume that an item classified as "trash" has no recycling or reuse option.

Waste management rules differ between locations. Check your local waste collection guidelines for the correct disposal method.

## Important Note
The AI WasteWise classifier predicts the category "trash" based on visual patterns learned from its training dataset. This does not mean that the item is universally non-recyclable or that it should always go into general waste.

If the model is uncertain, verify the item manually before disposal.
