# Plastic Waste

## What is it?
Plastic is a synthetic material commonly used for packaging, containers, bottles, bags, and many everyday products.

## Common Examples
- Plastic bottles
- Food containers
- Plastic packaging
- Plastic bags
- Plastic cups
- Plastic household containers

## Before Recycling
- Empty the container.
- Remove leftover food or liquid.
- Rinse it when appropriate.
- Keep recyclable plastic separate from general waste.
- Check the recycling symbol/type when available.

## Reuse Ideas
- Reuse suitable containers for storage.
- Use clean containers for organizing small items.
- Repurpose suitable plastic packaging for crafts or household uses.

## Disposal Guidance
Plastic recycling depends on the type of plastic and the facilities available in your area.
Do not assume that every plastic item is recyclable.

Check your local waste collection or recycling guidelines before disposal.
